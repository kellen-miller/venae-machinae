# Hobbyist vehicle-system design: competitive landscape

Research snapshot: 2026-08-31

## Question

Which current tools and official workflows serve hobbyist automotive wiring-harness and fuel, oil, or coolant-system design; where do their documented capabilities stop; and which workflow gaps should this MVP target?

## Answer

The reviewed hobbyist workflow is a toolchain, not one coherent design environment. Electrical work has capable harness-specific authoring tools ([harness.design](https://docs.harness.design/), [RapidHarness](https://rapidharness.com/support/design-types)), while custom fluid-system guidance is split across component calculators, compatibility selectors, and installation instructions ([DeatschWerks](https://deatschwerks.com/en-ca/pages/fuel-pump-calculator), [Earl's](https://www.holley.com/support/plumbing_an_fittings_and_hose/), [Davies Craig](https://daviescraig.com.au/media/2714/1709872790.UniversalEBPinstructions27Feb202428926.pdf)). Full 3D routed-system and lifecycle suites cover more of the engineering chain, but at the scope of mechanical assemblies, manufacturing, costing, and enterprise data management ([Autodesk](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Inventor-Help/files/GUID-9C802614-B78C-4555-83B9-D0FA032F8C6B.htm), [Siemens](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/technology/wire-harness-engineering/)).

The MVP should therefore own one narrow seam: a local-first, topology-first 2D project model spanning wiring plus fuel, oil, and coolant. The same model should drive operating-state overlays, domain-aware warnings and bounded calculations, lengths, and a BOM while preserving entered/calculated/unknown provenance. Generic drawing, arbitrary spreadsheet calculation, OEM repair content, comprehensive catalogs, 3D routing, manufacturing engineering, and full physical simulation are existing categories to reference or export to, not reproduce.

## Current workflows and their stop lines

| OEM repair reference | ALLDATA DIY provides vehicle-specific factory repair information, procedures, fluid specifications, component locations, and interactive wiring diagrams that expose connector/splice locations and circuit paths at system or component level ([product scope](https://www.alldata.com/diy-us/en/what-you-get)). | Its published workflow is selecting a vehicle and diagnosing, maintaining, or repairing that existing vehicle, not authoring a modified system ([vehicle workflow](https://www.alldata.com/diy-us/en/find-vehicle)). | Link evidence and let users trace an as-found baseline or modification delta; do not ingest or recreate subscription repair data. |
| Generic diagram + table | draw.io supplies shapes, fixed or floating connectors, waypoints, and layers ([connectors](https://www.drawio.com/docs/manual/connectors/), [layers](https://www.drawio.com/docs/manual/layers/)); Excel supplies structured tables/formulas and configurable input validation ([structured references](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Excel/using-structured-references-with-excel-tables), [validation](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/get-started/apply-data-validation-to-cells)). | These official workflows expose generic graphical and tabular primitives. Automotive meanings, cross-view consistency, units, and engineering rules remain work the user must encode; that is an inference from the documented primitives, not a claimed product defect. | Match their fast editing and CSV usefulness. | harness.design | The browser tool guides users through schematic design, harness layout, parts assignment, connection review, and export/share ([quick start](https://docs.harness.design/)). Reuse the principle that one connection edit updates every derived view, and extend it across thin electrical and fluid slices. |

Earl's selector recommends hose and hose-end families from fluid type and assembly method ([selector](https://www.holley.com/support/plumbing_an_fittings_and_hose/)). Setrab publishes oil-cooler recommendations and flow/heat-rejection specifications under use-case-specific input parameters ([oil-cooler workflow](https://setrabusa.com/collections/proline-std-oil-coolers)). ## MVP product boundary

### Target: the underserved seam

1. **One cross-domain project graph.** Model electrical components/ports/connections and fuel, oil, and coolant components/ports/connections without pretending the domains have identical rules. Show them on one optional vehicle background and let shared route geometry inform lengths.
2. **Operating-state understanding.** Let a user define states such as key-off, crank, run, fan-on, thermostat-bypass, or failure/degraded modes, then show electrical power/signal direction and fluid direction/temperature as explainable overlays. Stop at static, user-driven or bounded calculated states rather than circuit, CFD, or transient thermal simulation.
3. **Guided incompleteness.** Keep unknown values first-class. Every engineering value should be entered, calculated within a stated envelope, or unknown, with units, source/provenance, assumptions, and margin visible. Structural errors, incompatibilities, calculation warnings, and unknowns must not collapse into one generic alert.
4. **Modification workflow.** Capture the as-found reference, intended change, assumptions, selected parts, route measurements, and as-built verification in one project. External OEM or manufacturer documents remain linked evidence rather than copied catalogs.
5. **Single-source derivation.** A topology or property edit should update paths/overlays, applicable checks, lengths, and BOM/export data together. The product earns its place by eliminating cross-file drift, not by drawing prettier lines.

### Match only as necessary

- Fast 2D placement, ports, branching, route editing, labels, notes, lengths, user-defined parts, a BOM, and printable/data exports are baseline usability, already demonstrated by current harness tools ([harness.design exports](https://docs.harness.design/feature/export), [RapidHarness connections](https://rapidharness.com/support/design-types/harnesses/connections)).
- CSV/JSON/PDF output should make the project portable and interoperable. Advanced free-form diagramming and arbitrary formulas belong in draw.io and spreadsheets, which already provide those primitives ([draw.io CSV import](https://www.drawio.com/docs/manual/import/insert-from-csv/), [Excel formulas](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Excel/using-structured-references-with-excel-tables)).
- A small reusable user library is useful. A comprehensive commercial catalog is not a wedge; RapidHarness already advertises access to more than 80 million parts ([download page](https://rapidharness.com/download)).

### Explicitly defer

- OEM repair-information publication and interactive factory-diagram coverage.
- Detailed harness formboard/manufacturing engineering, NC outputs, costing, product variants, team administration, and enterprise lifecycle management.
- 3D cable/hose routing, bend/clearance or interference analysis, and mechanical CAD exchange.
- Full circuit simulation, transient thermal analysis, CFD, certification, and universal safety judgments.
- Vendor ordering, live pricing/inventory, and exhaustive manufacturer catalogs.

## Concrete validation bar

The competitive hypothesis is supported if one hobbyist can create one wiring subsystem and one fuel/oil/coolant subsystem in the same project; connect typed ports; route branches and record lengths over an optional vehicle background; switch named operating states; see direction/temperature overlays; enter values with units and provenance; distinguish errors, warnings, assumptions, and unknowns; and export a synchronized BOM plus printable/data artifacts without maintaining a parallel spreadsheet. If that workflow is not faster and clearer than a generic diagram plus table, the MVP has not reached the identified gap.

## Source limitations

- This review used public first-party product documentation, source repositories, and manufacturer guidance. It did not include hands-on trials, private sales demonstrations, user interviews, or community reports.
- Vendor pages describe their own products; capability claims were treated as documented scope, not independently benchmarked performance.
- A missing capability means it was absent from the reviewed official workflow, not that it is technically impossible or unavailable behind an undocumented/private feature.
- harness.design reached 1.0 on 2026-08-15 and WireViz warns that its functionality may change, so their boundaries should be rechecked before implementation or positioning decisions ([harness.design release](https://docs.harness.design/updates/1.0), [WireViz status](https://github.com/wireviz/WireViz)).
